About the Institution

ESC Government Polytechnic, Tekke, Nandyal is a government technical institution affiliated with the State Board of Technical Education and Training, Andhra Pradesh.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of technical education and produce committed skilled workforce using modern technology and contributing to industrial prosperity.

Our Mission

Provide effective learning experiences, practical exposure, ICT tools, life skills and continuous faculty development in an encouraging academic environment.

How admission works

Candidates seeking diploma admission appear for POLYCET conducted by SBTET AP. Admissions are based on POLYCET merit and applicable reservation rules.
